How CD-Forecast works

CD-Forecast estimates how much of each controlled medicine (opioids, psychotropic substances and precursors) Nigeria will need in future years: nationally, by geopolitical zone, by state, by facility type and by ownership.

The formula

1. Stock-out correction   B    = D × 365 / A
2. Need-based blend       M    = (1 − w)·B + w·N
3. Growth                 M · (1 + g)^n
4. Required quantity      Q(t) = M · (1 + g)^n · (1 + buffer) + add-on(t)

D quantity dispensed in the base year · A days in stock · g yearly growth (low/mid/high) · n years ahead · N need-based estimate · w weight on need · buffer safety mark-up (10% in the report) · add-on known extra demand.

Steps 1 and 4 follow the 2017 report (Sections 3.5 and 4.1). Steps 2 and 3 extend it to multi-year forecasting.

Data sources

  • 2017 survey (data 2014–16): all 36 states and FCT. Main method scales each state's sampled facilities to all facilities of that type in the state.
  • 2019 survey (data 2016–18): states sampled per zone (FCT and Lagos always included), facilities sampled by type, and patent medicine vendors (PPMVs) added. Main method scales by geopolitical zone, so it can be filtered by zone but not by state.
  • National average method (both surveys): national average per facility type × national number of that type.
  • My Data: your own yearly records, stock-out adjusted and scaled by "facilities represented".

Caveats

  • Survey data ends in 2018. Forecasts far beyond that are illustrative until newer records are added.
  • Three years cannot support a reliable growth rate. Plan to the low–high range, not one number.
  • Consumption-based forecasts repeat past patterns, including under-use (morphine) and possible diversion. Use the need blend where past use is known to be distorted.
  • 2019 survey, morphine: a single tertiary-hospital record accounts for about half of the estimate and is under review.
